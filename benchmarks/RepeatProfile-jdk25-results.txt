Reading: the unattributed samples are node equality, IntList.equals (Arrays.equals) reached through a
record's generated equals (ObjectMethods, a method handle). JFR's stack walk ends at the method-handle
frames, so these samples show no caller: 234 of them end in IntList.equals < Objects.equals <
DirectMethodHandle$Holder.invokeStatic, 118 in two LambdaForm frames. The previous file (70e7016) read
this as JFR's stack depth of 64; it is not: no sample is marked truncated, none has more than 16
frames, and neither stackdepth=256 nor -XX:+ShowHiddenFrames changed the share. Node equality is
called from the hashcons lookups (HashMap.getNode) in repair's re-keying and in add, and from the
list scans (ArrayList.indexOf) in repair and canonicalizeNodes; the matcher compares heads and
bindings, not whole nodes. So most of the two fifths belongs to rebuild, which is then the larger
half of the run, with the matcher at a quarter to a third. The split the profile can give: matcher at
least 26%, rebuild at least 26%, and node equality 40% that the two share with rebuild the main
owner. Since 70e7016 the deferred run went from 11.1 s to 9.2 s (RebuildBenchmark, one run) with
#31's limited search; the matcher's attributed share went from 32.4% to 26.5%.
