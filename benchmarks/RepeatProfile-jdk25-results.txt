Reading: the run is a little shorter (1.5 s against 1.6 s) and the matcher's share fell again, from
44% to 34%, as a pattern node came to read its candidates from the class's table without touching a
node object (#86) and the walk stopped paying for each search's lists (#93); in the matcher's top of
stack the table's binary search (EClass$Table.lower) now appears at 4.7%. Rebuild is 20% and apply
16%, so the two phases outside the matcher are now more than half of what is attributed. With 128
samples each line is worth 0.8%, so the shares are coarse. The unattributed share (30%, up from
22%) is the samples whose outer frames JFR cut off at its stack depth: in part the test's
checkInvariants after the run (EGraph.checkParentEntries, 5.5% of all samples) and the hashing and
comparison of nodes through record equality, whose frames are deep (the top of stack there is
DirectMethodHandle$Holder, Objects.equals and Objects.hashCode, 7% together); the rest is not
named. The phases' shares are therefore lower bounds. The profile of the fuzz terms on #74, from a
recording with thousands of samples, is the better reading of where time goes now.
