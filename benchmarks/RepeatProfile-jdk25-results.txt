Reading: the run is half what it was (1.6 s against 3 s) and what left was the matcher's: its share
fell from 81% to 44% once a pattern node visited only the nodes with its head (#80, the index), the
deduplication set went where no duplicate can arise (#81) and the per-candidate allocations were
pooled or chained (#82). With 135 samples each line is worth 0.7%, so the shares are coarse. Of
what remains, the matcher's time is in matchIn and matchChildren themselves - loading a candidate
node and its children - which #74's next experiment, the class table, addresses; rebuild is now
the second phase at 22%, in repair's hashcons re-keying and the canonicalisation of each parent
entry (IntList.map). EGraph.checkParentEntries is the test's checkInvariants after the run, not
the saturation; the unattributed fifth is mostly that check and the runner's own frames.
